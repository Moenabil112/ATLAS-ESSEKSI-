## 1. Core Architectural Position

The current capital architecture is built around a limited and private financial partner entry into a dedicated **Isseksi ProjectCo / SPV**.

The structure is not a public offering, not a sale of existing founder shares, not a sale of mining licenses, and not a transfer of Akanil / HYRION technology.

The selected structure is:

**Capital Increase into ProjectCo**

A Moroccan financial partner with industrial understanding enters the ProjectCo through new capital injected into the company. The contribution is dedicated to the current expansion phase of the Isseksi Green Copper Production Platform.

---

## 2. Strategic Intent

The purpose of the 10% financial partner layer is to finance the current operational expansion of the Isseksi project without transferring control.

The layer is designed to:

1. Bring disciplined expansion liquidity.
2. Finance the current USD 3.6M expansion requirement.
3. Support deployment of the mobile green copper production model.
4. Prepare quarry development and operational readiness.
5. Build credibility for later strategic financing rounds.
6. Preserve Founder / Sponsor control.
7. Protect Atlas Mining’s license position.
8. Protect Akanil / HYRION technology and data architecture.
9. Avoid premature entry of controlling industrial or offtake investors.
10. Establish a clean, transparent, and reviewable ProjectCo structure.

## 6. Payment Structure — Three Clear Tranches

The USD 3.6M contribution is divided into three transparent payments.

| Tranche | Amount | % of Total | Timing | Main Purpose | Release Condition |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| Tranche 1 | USD 1.20M | 33.33% | At signing / first closing | ProjectCo activation, governance setup, initial technical readiness, initial procurement planning | Signed shareholder agreement, ProjectCo account, approved Use of Funds, initial governance protocol |
| Tranche 2 | USD 1.20M | 33.33% | After Gate 1 | Mobile station procurement / development, quarry preparation, operating setup | Report on Tranche 1 use, technical execution report, approved procurement and quarry plan |
| Tranche 3 | USD 1.20M | 33.34% | After Gate 2 | Ramp-up, working capital, operational readiness, production scale-up | Operational readiness report, quarterly review, updated budget, board / ProjectCo approval |

**Total Contribution:** USD 3.6M

## 7. Equity Recognition Against Payments

The investor’s 10% stake is recognized progressively against paid capital.

| Payment | Capital Paid | Equity Recognized |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tranche 1 | USD 1.20M | 3.33% |
| Tranche 2 | USD 1.20M | Additional 3.33% |
| Tranche 3 | USD 1.20M | Additional 3.34% |
| Total | USD 3.60M | 10.00% |

If a tranche is not funded, the investor retains only the equity corresponding to the capital actually paid, subject to the final shareholder agreement.

Once the full USD 3.6M is paid and the 10% is fully recognized, the partner’s stake remains preserved according to the shareholder agreement.

## 9. Quarterly Monitoring Rights

The financial partner receives structured review rights every three months.

Quarterly review covers:

| Review Area | Content |
|---|---|
| Financial | Capital deployed, cash balance, budget versus actual |
| Technical | Mobile station status, quarry preparation, QA/QC updates |
| Operational | Readiness, equipment, team, production preparation |
| ESG / Water | Water use, monitoring, environmental controls |
| Data Room | Updated documents, reporting discipline, access logs |
| Risk Register | Open risks, mitigations, corrective actions |
| Next Quarter | Budget, priorities, execution milestones |

The partner has the right to review, ask questions, and request clarification.  
The partner does not receive day-to-day operational control.

## 11. Reserved Matters

The following matters should require investor notification or approval depending on final negotiation:

1. Change of ProjectCo purpose.
2. Use of funds outside approved expansion budget.
3. New debt above an agreed threshold.
4. Sale or pledge of core ProjectCo assets.
5. Material change to the mobile station strategy.
6. Issuance of new equity affecting investor dilution.
7. Material related-party transactions.
8. Exclusive offtake agreement affecting ProjectCo economics.
9. Transfer of operating rights or material contractual rights.
10. Change to ProjectCo governance structure.

## 12. Future Round Priority

The financial partner receives a priority participation right in future financing rounds.

Recommended clause logic:

**The Financial Partner shall have a priority participation right in future financing rounds, subject to compliance with its obligations and without creating exclusivity over the company’s future capital strategy.**

Arabic interpretation:

يتمتع الشريك المالي بحق أولوية المشاركة في الجولات التمويلية اللاحقة، بشرط التزامه بتعهداته، ودون أن يشكل ذلك حقًا حصريًا أو قيدًا على استراتيجية رأس المال المستقبلية للشركة.

This right is a participation priority, not an exclusivity right and not a veto over future financing.

## 13. Founder / Sponsor Protection Rules

The architecture must preserve the following protections:

1. No sale of existing founder shares.
2. Capital enters ProjectCo only.
3. Atlas Mining’s license position is protected.
4. Akanil / HYRION technology remains licensed or contracted, not transferred.
5. Investor receives equity only against actual paid capital.
6. Investor rights are governance and review rights, not operational control.
7. Data room access is controlled and permission-based.
8. No automatic offtake exclusivity.
9. No automatic blocking right over ordinary operations.
10. No transfer of control to the financial partner.
11. No unrestricted access to geological, technical, or strategic data.
12. No dilution outside the agreed shareholder framework.
